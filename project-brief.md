# GeoRisk – Project Brief

## The question
Which settlements in Akure South Local Government Area sit in low-lying areas near watercourses?

## The data I need
- Watercourses – OpenStreetMap (via QuickOSM) – extracted for Akure South, Ondo State, Nigeria – 31 stream segments confirmed
- LGA/administrative boundaries – Nigeria Subnational Administrative Boundaries (COD-AB), OCHA – https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-nga – GeoJSON – ~10.9 MB
- Settlement extents – GRID3 NGA Settlement Extents v3.1 – https://data.humdata.org/dataset/grid3-nga-settlement-extents-v3-1 – GeoPackage – ~702 MB
- Elevation – Copernicus DEM GLO-30 (30m) – https://portal.opentopography.org
